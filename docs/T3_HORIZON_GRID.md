# T3 expanded finite-horizon and close-approach gate

This optional audit follows the two synthetic T3N mass configurations beyond the earlier 0.35-period check. A feasibility preflight on 2026-09-27 examined several perturbations and horizons in the pinned SciPy environment. It found that a momentum-balanced tangential velocity perturbation of $0.02\omega a$ remains separated through 0.75 of the unperturbed circular period, whereas both configurations cross a pair-separation threshold of $0.5a$ by one period. The frozen validation grid therefore includes the two 0.75-period cases and the two one-period stress cases. The inherited `train` and `holdout` labels describe the source fixture; grid selection used this preflight, so these rows are **not** an untouched scientific holdout.

The smooth cases compare local velocity-Verlet at a coarse and doubled step count, independently coded Cartesian RK4 at the fine count, and pinned SciPy 1.18.1 DOP853 with relative tolerance $10^{-12}$ and absolute tolerance $10^{-14}$. The local step count scales from the prior 0.35-period fixture to preserve approximately the same step duration. Position error is divided by the initial side $a$, and velocity error by $\omega a$. The gates are fine-Verlet position and velocity errors at most $10^{-5}$, RK4 position error at most $10^{-8}$, coarse/fine Verlet position-error ratio at least 3, relative energy drift at most $10^{-5}$, angular-momentum and barycenter drift at most $10^{-10}$, no detected inward $0.5a$ separation event, and sampled minimum separation above $0.5a$.

The stress cases run the same DOP853 event monitor to one period. At least one inward $0.5a$ crossing must be detected in each; they are recorded as **excluded**, with no solver-accuracy claim at their final states. Pair separations are also sampled at 501 times, but event detection supplies the crossing guard between those samples. This is a numerical event check, not a mathematical guarantee that no unobserved approach exists.

The declared compute budget is at most 12,000 DOP853 right-hand-side calls across all four cases and 35,000 fixed steps across coarse Verlet, fine Verlet, and RK4 in the two admitted cases. These counts are deterministic in the pinned environment. Wall time varies by machine and is diagnostic rather than a pass gate. `python scripts/verify_t3_horizon_grid.py --verify` recomputes the [receipt](../artifacts/t3-horizon-grid-audit.json) and checks source bytes, numerical gates, and the excluded stress cases. The pinned wheel and installed-license hashes are checked by the existing SciPy environment verifier.

The earlier `t3-perturbed-audit.json` was generated with CRLF line endings in this Windows checkout, while Git stores it as LF under the repository text attribute. Its source fingerprint in this receipt explicitly normalizes CRLF to LF before hashing and counting bytes. Every other source path is hashed as raw bytes. This keeps the same content binding in the working tree and a fresh Git archive.

This extends a smooth, synthetic numerical cross-check only to 0.75 period and shows why the one-period cases are outside its admission gate. It does not establish chaotic long-horizon accuracy, general three-body correctness, collision handling, real ephemerides or mission validity, or scientific publication readiness. The core T3/T3N Runs do not depend on SciPy.
