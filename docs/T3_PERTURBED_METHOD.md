# T3 finite-horizon perturbed three-body audit

This optional audit extends the symmetric T3N initial states to two synthetic, momentum-balanced velocity perturbations. It compares a local velocity-Verlet integration with independently coded Cartesian RK4 and the pinned SciPy 1.18.1 `solve_ivp(method="DOP853")` reference. SciPy and NumPy remain outside the core package and its T3/T3N Runs; the existing [external solver audit](T3_EXTERNAL_SOLVER.md) checks installed versions and license-file hashes and records the pinned wheel hashes and audited source revisions.

The two predeclared cases reuse the equal-mass train and unequal-mass holdout T3N fixture; these inherited labels do not denote training or parameter fitting in this audit. Body 0 receives a tangential velocity increment $\epsilon\omega a$ and body 1 receives $-m_0\epsilon\omega a/m_1$, so the barycentric linear momentum remains zero. The values of $\epsilon$ are 0.02 and 0.03, respectively. Both runs end at 0.35 of the corresponding unperturbed circular period. Verlet is run at the fixture step count and twice that count; RK4 uses the fine step count. The DOP853 relative and absolute tolerances are $10^{-12}$ and $10^{-14}$. No softening or collision regularization is applied.

All position errors are divided by the initial triangle side length $a$; velocity errors are divided by $\omega a$. The gates require fine-Verlet endpoint position and velocity errors $\le 10^{-4}$, RK4 endpoint position error $\le 10^{-7}$, coarse/fine Verlet position-error ratio $\ge 3$, relative energy drift $\le 10^{-4}$, relative angular-momentum drift $\le 10^{-10}$, barycenter drift $\le 10^{-10}a$, sampled pair separation $\ge 0.5a$, and deviation from the unperturbed analytic orbit $\ge 10^{-4}a$. A repulsive-force Verlet calculation on the holdout case must differ from the attractive DOP853 result by at least $0.1a$. The committed [receipt](../artifacts/t3-perturbed-audit.json) includes the two rows, all gates, source-file SHA-256 hashes, and provider environment evidence.

Run `python scripts/verify_t3_perturbed.py --verify` inside the pinned SciPy environment. Verification recomputes the numerical results and compares them with the committed receipt. These cases avoid close approaches and cover only a short, smooth interval. Agreement among three implementations and conservation diagnostics support this bounded numerical calculation; they do not establish long-horizon stability, chaotic-regime accuracy, real ephemeris or mission validity, general N-body correctness, or a core replayable T3P Run. Those remain separate gates.
